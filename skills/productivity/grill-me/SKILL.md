Call the Skill tool with "plan-first", then with "grilling".

Grill as `grilling` says, within `plan-first`'s cap. When the grilling is about something to build (a feature, a fix, a change to code), do not stop at shared understanding: carry on with `plan-first` from its step 2 (acceptance tests, stop 1) through plan mode and the codex plan review to stop 2. When it is only an idea or a decision with nothing to build, stop when the frontier is empty, as `grilling` says.
