# Inbox Assist

Inbox Assist is a Slack app that helps a support team reply to customer email: a workspace installs it and connects one Gmail mailbox; when a new customer email arrives (via Gmail Pub/Sub push), the app drafts three reply options with an LLM and posts them to the installing user's Slack DM, where a person can send one option as a threaded Gmail reply, edit a draft in a modal and send it, or dismiss it, after which the Slack message shows who sent or dismissed it; machine mail and the team's own sent replies are skipped, support can ask whether a given sender's email got a reply, and uninstalling the app disconnects the workspace. It must be multi-tenant (many Slack workspaces and mailboxes in one deployment, with no data or actions crossing between them), concurrency-safe across several app instances sharing one database, idempotent on sends (each approved reply goes out exactly once despite retries, duplicate deliveries, double clicks and crashes), secure against forged, replayed or hostile input (including email content aimed at the LLM), and it must acknowledge every Slack request within Slack's 3 second deadline.
